Startup Expansion Analysis : Bangalore → Hyderabad
Overview :
This project analyzes a startup’s expansion from Bangalore to Hyderabad using data-driven insights. It focuses on revenue, cost, profit, and demand trends to identify the best expansion city.
Objective :
Compare Bangalore vs Hyderabad performance
Analyze revenue, cost, and profit
Identify best expansion market
Support business decision-making
Tools Used :
Excel
Power BI
DAX
Dashboard Highlights :
Orders trend comparison
Revenue vs cost analysis
Profit comparison by city
Cost breakdown
Revenue distribution
Key Insights : 
Hyderabad shows higher growth potential
Lower operational cost improves profit
Bangalore is more saturated
Hyderabad is better for expansion
Risks :
Market competition      
Demand uncertainty
Scaling challenges
Conclusion :
Hyderabad is the recommended expansion city due to better growth and profitability.
