🚀 Startup Expansion Analysis: Bangalore vs Hyderabad

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the feasibility of expanding a startup from Bangalore to Hyderabad using a data-driven approach. The dashboard compares both cities based on key business metrics such as revenue, cost, profit, and customer demand, helping stakeholders identify the most profitable expansion market.
The analysis aims to support strategic decision-making by evaluating operational performance and growth opportunities in both cities.

🎯 Objectives
Compare the performance of Bangalore and Hyderabad.
Analyze revenue, costs, profits, and order trends.
Identify the city with better growth potential.
Support expansion decisions using business intelligence.
Understand operational and marketing cost structures.

🛠 Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and cleaning
Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Calculated measures and KPIs

📊 Dashboard Features
1. Revenue vs Cost Analysis
Monthly comparison of total revenue and total cost.
Helps evaluate profitability trends over time.
2. Profit Comparison by City
Displays overall profit generated by Bangalore and Hyderabad.
Enables direct performance comparison.
3. Monthly Order Trends
Tracks customer demand and order volume across both cities.
Identifies seasonal patterns and growth opportunities.
4. Cost Breakdown
Analysis of:
Rent Cost
Marketing Cost
Delivery Cost per Order
5. Revenue Distribution
Percentage contribution of each city to total revenue.
6. KPI Cards
Total Revenue: ₹397M
Total Cost: ₹67M

📈 Key Insights
Hyderabad generated higher revenue and profit compared to Bangalore.
Hyderabad recorded higher monthly order volumes, indicating stronger customer demand.
Operational costs remained relatively controlled, improving profitability.
Bangalore appears to be a more mature and competitive market.
Hyderabad demonstrates greater potential for future growth and expansion.

⚠ Challenges & Risks
Intense market competition.
Uncertainty in future demand.
Scaling and operational management challenges.
Marketing and customer acquisition costs may increase over time.
Need for continuous monitoring of market trends.

📋 Business Recommendation
Based on the analysis, Hyderabad emerges as the more attractive expansion market due to:
✔ Higher revenue generation
✔ Better profitability
✔ Stronger customer demand
✔ Greater growth opportunities

✅ Conclusion
The Startup Expansion Analysis Dashboard provides valuable insights into the comparative performance of Bangalore and Hyderabad. The findings indicate that Hyderabad offers better growth prospects and profitability, making it the preferred city for strategic expansion.
